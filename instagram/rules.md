# Instagram rules for Reel edits

These rules apply every time a Reel is edited or scripted through the Video-lab pipeline
(talking-head-reel-editor or any other edit).

1. **Score the script with instagram-agent.** Before choosing the opening, score the hook
   and spoken lines using the instagram-agent hook scoring (Reel / hooks mode). Show the
   scores and pick the strongest line as the opening.
2. **Write the caption with instagram-agent.** Every Reel's caption is written with
   instagram-agent caption mode, in Swati's voice (see `instagram/voice.md`), with up to
   5 hashtags.
3. **Nothing is posted automatically.** Hooks, scripts and captions are drafts for Swati
   to approve and post herself.
