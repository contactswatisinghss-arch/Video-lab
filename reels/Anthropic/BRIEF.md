# Anthropic v2: a $3.4B bet on power (AI is a 5-layer cake)

**Why v2:** Swati found v1 monotonous: one device (a dark panel dropping from the top) repeated ~10 times, and step 3
research was skipped. v2 uses a different visual device on every beat; research in `RESEARCH.md`.

**Audience:** curious non-techies who use ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini. **Type:** news explainer.
**Angle:** while AI stocks crashed, Claude's maker spent big, not on a model or chips but on electricity.
**Payoff:** AI is a 5-layer cake; energy is the bottom layer, and that's where the next AI battle is.

## Length and cuts (unchanged from v1)
Raw 112 s → about 75 s (speed 1.1×, pauses > 0.3 s cut). Cut the slow setup, "Think about it", and the whole
NVIDIA-founder/Jensen Huang attribution (Swati). Whisper fixes: "$3 .4" → $3.4, "1 .7" → 1.7. `hdr: original`.

## Look
Her real daytime room, original camera look. Palette: electric amber #FFB020 = power, red = crash/risk,
green = what works, plus one bold colour per carousel card (violet = models, teal = GPUs). Sora + JetBrains Mono.
