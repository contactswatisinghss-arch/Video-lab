## What we take
- Promise the HOW in the hook and deliver it as numbered steps (refs 1, 2).
- Everyday analogies for every technical idea (ref 1, 2), but our own: standing order, token number in a bank queue.
- A graphic change every 2-3 s; chain diagram for the steps.
- Our gap nobody filled: the leadership lesson (access vs. authority to move money) and, in Part 2, why crypto exists at all.

## What we avoid
- Unverified claims (ref 3's "identified" + MetaMask story; ref 1's "$1 in, $2B out" and "4 hours" specifics).
- Pure headline recaps (ref 4).
