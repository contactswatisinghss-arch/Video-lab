# NuclearFusion: brief + storyboard (v1, awaiting Swati's approval)

**Series:** AI breakthroughs that could change the future, Episode 1. **Type:** news explainer.
**Audience:** curious non-experts + techies; the stakes are "AI isn't only taking jobs, it's solving decades-old problems".
**Angle:** a mini sun on Earth is real science; the hard part is holding it steady, and AI now reacts faster than humans.
**Payoff:** "stop being scared of AI, start learning it", plus follow for episode 2.

  goes from ~2.5 to ~2.8 words/s, still under the refs' 3.4–3.9).

  - "And this could transform the way we power the world" (repeat)
- Still 100 s, above the 35–90 sweet spot. Option: split into 2 parts (Part 1 ends on "That's where AI comes in").
  My pick: keep one Reel, because the visuals change every ~2 s and the AI payoff is the reason to watch.
- **Hook check:** her spoken opener scores 12–29/100 (hookscore.py; under 50 = weak: no number, no tension, no "you").
  Best re-record option scored **62**: *"You're scared of AI, but it's helping keep a 100 million degree sun stable."*
  10 s on camera would beat any edit; if not, the frame-0 title + sun visual carry the hook.
